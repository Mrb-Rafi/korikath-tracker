Size by

The mother category is in the centre and each ring is one level deeper. Click a slice to zoom into that cluster; click the centre to go back up. Each slice is drawn under the category the tracker found it through first; a category or file filed in several places is counted in each, so slice sizes can add up to more than the unique total.

Click a circle or name to open or close it · ↗ opens the category on Commons · bigger circle = more files · biggest branches first

Crawls

Quick check (about 3 min) reads only what changed since the last crawl. Full crawl (about 13 min) re-reads every category and also catches renamed files. On-wiki usage (about 20 min) checks every file's use on other Wikimedia wikis. Either runs in the background: you can close this page and it keeps going.
